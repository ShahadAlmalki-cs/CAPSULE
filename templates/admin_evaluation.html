Metric Definitions

MetricFormulaInterpretation
Top-1 Accuracy Correct top predictions / Total queries The predicted drug at rank-1 exactly matches the expected drug.
Top-5 Accuracy Queries where expected appears in top-5 / Total queries The correct drug appears somewhere in the top-5 results.
Precision (micro) TP / (TP + FP) Of all drugs the model predicted, what fraction were correct.
Recall (micro) TP / (TP + FN) Of all actual drugs in the test set, what fraction were retrieved correctly.
F1-Score 2 × Precision × Recall / (Precision + Recall) Harmonic mean — balances precision and recall.
AUC (proxy) (Avg confcorrect + (1 − Avg confwrong)) / 2 Approximates ranking quality. 1.0 = perfect separation; 0.5 = random.
