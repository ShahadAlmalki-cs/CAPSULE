What is CAPSULE?

CAPSULE is an AI-powered medication identification and management system developed at Taif University as a Senior Capstone Project. It helps doctors, pharmacists, and patients identify medications from text descriptions, check medicine interactions, and manage long-term medication safety.

The system uses natural language processing, TF-IDF-based similarity matching, machine learning support, and a curated medication database to provide ranked, context-aware medication suggestions.
