Performance Metrics — AI / ML / DL
Weighted-average scores on held-out test split · target: dosage_form
Benchmark
Accuracy
Fraction of all predictions the model got correct. Simple and intuitive — but can be misleading on imbalanced classes.
Formula: (TP + TN) / Total
F1-Score
Harmonic mean of Precision and Recall. Best metric when you need balance between false positives and false negatives.
Formula: 2 × (P × R) / (P + R)
Precision / Recall
Precision: of all positive predictions, how many were actually correct. Recall: of all real positives, how many did the model catch.
P = TP/(TP+FP) · R = TP/(TP+FN)
AUC-ROC
Area Under the ROC Curve — measures the model's ability to discriminate between classes at all thresholds. 1.0 = perfect · 0.5 = random.
Higher is always better (max 1.0)
